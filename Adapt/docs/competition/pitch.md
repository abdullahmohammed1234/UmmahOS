# 30-second pitch

Most AI tutors focus on whether an answer is correct.

A correct answer can come from guessing, while an incorrect answer can come from a minor mistake.

ADAPT models evidence about how the learner arrived at an answer and uses that evidence to choose the next instructional strategy.

Different learner evidence produces different decisions, and the workflow has been stress-tested across multiple benchmark phases. Human learning impact has not been established: Phase 5 is INCONCLUSIVE with n = 0.
