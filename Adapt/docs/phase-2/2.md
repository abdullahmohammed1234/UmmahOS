## 1. Motivation

Phase 1F showed that ADAPT's evidence-driven core generalizes reasonably well (development 39/42 = 92.9%, holdout 17/18 = 94.4%, gap −1.6 pp, **ROBUST**). It also exposed three genuine limitations:

- **G-001-B:** fraction subtraction did not accumulate to INCREASE after three successes.
- **G-003-A/B:** a delayed misconception after a strong streak was treated as global regression (`DECREASE`).
- **G-005-D (holdout):** learner *state* recovered (mastery rose) but instructional *strategy* stayed `REMEDIATE`.

Those failures are not labeling bugs. They show that **what the learner knows** and **what the tutor should do next** were collapsed into one AdaptationEngine decision.

---

## 2. Phase 1F Findings

Frozen Phase 1F outcome: **ROBUST**. Four scored failures were kept:

| ID | Split | Phase 1F decision | Type | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G-001-B | development | MAINTAIN | GENERALIZATION_FAILURE | LOW |
| G-003-A | development | DECREASE | MISCONCEPTION_PERSISTENCE | MEDIUM |
| G-003-B | development | DECREASE | MISCONCEPTION_PERSISTENCE | MEDIUM |
| G-005-D | holdout | REMEDIATE | RECOVERY_FAILURE | HIGH |

Cause: the Phase 1D engine treats `REGRESSING + consecutive negatives` as decrease, and `repeated_misconceptions` as remediate even after later strong correct work if records are not marked `RESOLVED`.

---

## 3. Research Question

Can ADAPT distinguish **what is happening to the learner** from **what instructional strategy should happen next**—and recover that strategy when the learner recovers?

## 5. Strategy State

`StrategyState` is explicit and separate from mastery:

- `current_strategy` / `previous_strategy`
- `strategy_confidence`
- `transition_reason` / `transition_evidence`
- misconception flag (`FLAGGED` / `CLEARED`)
- hysteresis fields (`last_extreme_strategy`, `steps_since_extreme`)
- recovery counters

Required names: `ASSESS`, `PROBE`, `MAINTAIN`, `INCREASE`, `DECREASE`, `REMEDIATE`, `RECOVER`, `GATHER_EVIDENCE`.

`RECOVER` is internal. The exposed action after recovery is `MAINTAIN` or `PROBE`, never automatic `REMEDIATE → INCREASE`.

Selector mapping reuses existing `AdaptationAction` values (`PROBE` → `PROBE_UNCERTAINTY`, etc.).

---

## 6. Strategy Transitions

Transitions consider current/previous strategy, learner state, recent and historical evidence, misconception status, mastery trend, confidence/uncertainty, response sequence, and difficulty mismatch.

Examples:

- `REMEDIATE` + one correct → stay `REMEDIATE`
- `REMEDIATE` + two or more quality successes → internal `RECOVER` → `MAINTAIN`/`PROBE`
- Strong history + isolated misconception → `PROBE`
- Repeated misconception (≥ 3, configurable) → `REMEDIATE`
- Established global regression → `DECREASE`

---

## 7. Misconception vs Regression

Not an unconditional `new misconception = PROBE` rule.

- Isolated misconception + strong prior + limited evidence → `PROBE` / `GATHER_EVIDENCE`
- Repeated misconception → `REMEDIATE`
- Consecutive weak failures, low confidence, no strong prior → `DECREASE` / `GATHER_EVIDENCE`

This is the G-003 fix.

---

## 8. Strategy Recovery

State recovery (mastery ↑) is not strategy recovery.

Recovery requires a streak of successful responses **and** reasoning/confidence quality. Correctness alone is not enough. After recovery, remediation does not persist without new misconception evidence.

This is the G-005 fix.

---

## 9. Cross-Concept Generalization

The strategy package does not branch on `basic_algebra` or `fractions`. Concept-specific cues remain in challenge banks. Strategy consumes evidence/state.

Evaluated on both algebra (31 scenarios) and fractions (29 scenarios).

G-001-B is **not** recoded to INCREASE. `FR-M-002` is subtraction; the supplied reasoning discusses adding numerators, so evidence is moderate. Phase 1F's INCREASE expectation was too strong for that item.

---

## 10. Hysteresis / Stability

`INCREASE` followed by one error does not automatically `DECREASE`. `DECREASE` followed by one success does not automatically `INCREASE`. Ambiguous evidence prefers `PROBE` or `GATHER_EVIDENCE`. Established regression can still decrease after enough negative evidence.

---

## 11. Decision Trace

When the strategy engine is enabled, each trace includes:

Response → Evidence → State Update → Strategy Transition → Decision → Challenge

Fields: `decision`, `reason`, `current_strategy`, `previous_strategy`, `evidence_ids`, `state_snapshot`, `confidence`, `transition`.

## 16. Limitations

- Keyword/heuristic analyzer is unchanged; strategy quality is bounded by evidence quality.
- Recovery and persistence thresholds are configurable but still discrete rules.
- Wilson intervals for n=4 and n=8 are wide.
- `RECOVER` is internal; challenge selection still uses the Phase 1 action vocabulary.
- No LLM. Later phases may use this layer as a baseline/guardrail.

---

## 17. Conclusion

ADAPT can now estimate learner state **and** decide when instructional strategy should change.

The strongest evidence is the Phase 1F failure path:

1. G-003 delayed misconception → `PROBE` instead of `DECREASE`
2. G-005 successful remediation → strategy recovers to `MAINTAIN`
3. G-001 cross-concept → no hardcoded concept names; INCREASE only when evidence is actually strong

Phase 1D–1F successful behavior is preserved.

Hypothesis for this phase: **supported on the dedicated regressions and the new Phase 2 suite**, without claiming a valid overall-percentage improvement over Phase 1F.
