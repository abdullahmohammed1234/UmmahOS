---

## 1. Objective

Determine whether ADAPT's adaptive behavior generalizes beyond the contract-aligned Phase 1E scenarios.

---

## 2. Hypothesis

**H1:** ADAPT's evidence-driven adaptation generalizes to unseen learner scenarios and remains appropriately sensitive to relevant evidence without becoming unstable or overconfident.

**H0:** ADAPT's adaptive behavior does not generalize reliably beyond the Phase 1E distribution.

---

## 3. Relationship to Phase 1E

Phase 1E remains the historical benchmark. No Phase 1E scenario, label, metric definition, or result file is edited in this phase.

Phase 1F adds a fractions challenge bank in `benchmarks/phase1f/challenge_bank.py`. The Phase 1D algebra bank is unchanged.

---

## 4. Threats to Generalization

Concept lock-in, template matching on algebra wording, inability to handle delayed/alternating misconceptions, overreaction to noise, failure to recover after remediation, instruction-following from learner text, and state drift on long histories.

---

## 5. Scenario Design

15 families × 4 variants = 60 scored executions. Families G-001–G-015 as specified (concept transfer, multi-step, delayed misconception, temporary confusion, recovery, failed remediation, confidence mismatch, confidence collapse, correct/wrong explanation, incorrect/strong method, alternating misconceptions, multi-signal conflict, medium-term stability, distribution shift, adversarial).

At least 50% are tagged novel. At least 30% combine three or more evidence dimensions.

## 12. Statistical Method

Wilson 95% intervals via the Phase 1E helper. Gap = development rate − holdout rate. No post-hoc threshold tuning. No significance claim from a single percentage difference.

## 13. Frozen Evaluation Rules

Interpretation bands (assigned after execution, not altered afterward):

- **ROBUST:** holdout ≥ 0.80, gap ≤ 0.10, metamorphic ≥ 0.80, adversarial instruction tests all block override, recovery ≥ 0.70
- **PARTIALLY_ROBUST:** holdout ≥ 0.65 and gap ≤ 0.20, or holdout ≥ 0.50 with meaningful weaknesses
- **NOT_ROBUST:** holdout < 0.50, or core adaptive claim fails under generalization

## 15. Failure Analysis

Four scored failures. None were removed.

| ID | Split | Decision | Type | Severity | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G-001-B | development | MAINTAIN | GENERALIZATION_FAILURE | LOW | Fraction subtraction cues did not accumulate to INCREASE after three strong-ish successes. Domain transfer is incomplete for this item, not a collapse. |
| G-003-A | development | DECREASE | MISCONCEPTION_PERSISTENCE | MEDIUM | Four strong algebra successes then two delayed misconceptions were treated as regression (DECREASE) rather than probe/gather. |
| G-003-B | development | DECREASE | MISCONCEPTION_PERSISTENCE | MEDIUM | Same structure on fractions. Delayed misconception currently looks like a difficulty drop. |
| G-005-D | **holdout** | REMEDIATE | RECOVERY_FAILURE | HIGH | Mastery rose after post-misconception correct work, but the decision remained REMEDIATE. Recovery of *state* ≠ recovery of *strategy* when misconception records are not marked RESOLVED. |

Cause classification: not a crash, not a Phase 1E contract rewrite, not scenario-label cheating. These are **genuine generalization / boundary limitations** of the Phase 1D updater/engine (regression rule vs delayed misconception; resolve-misconception requires STRONG+HIGH reliability on the recovery items).

No ADAPT code was changed in response.

## 16. Development vs Holdout

Development 92.9% vs holdout 94.4%. Gap −1.6 pp. Holdout Wilson interval is wide (n=18). No evidence that the development set was used to overfit the engine.

---

## 17. Limitations

- Keyword/heuristic analyzer; no LLM.
- Phase 1D analyzer cannot attach `misconception_signal` to a correct answer (G-009 is scored on “do not INCREASE,” not on conceptual error type).
- Fractions exist only in the Phase 1F bank.
- Longitudinal mastery can saturate at 1.0 after long successful tails.
- M-012 denominator includes persistence-tagged families; 0% means no recovery case stayed stuck solely by that flag.
- Small family n; Wilson intervals are wide.

---

## 18. Conclusion

**Does ADAPT generalize beyond the Phase 1E contract suite?**

**Yes, with documented boundaries.** Holdout 94.4% vs development 92.9%, metamorphic and adversarial suites passed, fractions concept transfer mostly worked, learner instructions did not override rules, and 20-step trajectories stayed bounded and traceable.

The important discovered boundary: **delayed misconceptions can trigger DECREASE (regression) instead of diagnostic probing**, and **one holdout recovery case kept REMEDIATE after mastery had already improved**. Those are valuable Phase 1F findings, not hidden.

Hypothesis: **H1 is supported at the pre-registered ROBUST band**, not as 100% perfection.
