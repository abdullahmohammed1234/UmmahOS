# Phase 9 — Competitive Product Polish

**Project:** ADAPT  
**Phase:** 9  
**Status:** COMPLETE — PASS  
**Seed:** `20260815` (Phase 9 benchmark and demo). Historical engine seed remains `20260814`.

The adaptive engine was not redesigned. Historical Phase 1–8 artifacts were not rewritten. Phase 5 remains INCONCLUSIVE (n=0). No educational-efficacy claim was added.

---

## Objective

Turn the validated Phase 8 prototype into a competition-ready learning product:

Choose → Learn → Answer → ADAPT notices → Adaptation → Improve

without replacing:

Learner Response → Evidence → Learner State → Strategy → Next Challenge

---

## What Changed

Product and catalog only:

- Visual identity: dark hero, subject-colored cards, cleaner challenge/feedback
- Lightweight evidence: 3-tap confidence, approach chips, optional explanation mapped into the existing response contract
- Stronger product-level challenge rotation (`HARD_WINDOW=4`, score penalty over 8)
- Expanded catalog: 7 domains, ≥10 concepts each, 21 challenge types
- Honesty labels on progress (Explored / Building confidence / Strong evidence)
- Journey stages, insights, recommendations from recorded traces/state
- Research Mode remains optional; counterfactual remains two live `AdaptiveTutor` runs

Frozen and unmodified in decision logic:

- EvidenceAnalyzer
- StateUpdater
- AdaptiveStrategyEngine
- AdaptationEngine
- AdaptiveTutor (default bank and selector)

---

## Visual Improvements

Landing immediately shows **ADAPT**, **Learn differently.**, the product promise, **Start Learning**, and a seven-subject explore grid.

Challenge screen: question, structured answer, three confidence taps, optional approach, optional explanation, **Check Answer**.

Feedback: short result, **What ADAPT noticed**, **Why this question?**, then Your response → ADAPT noticed → Your next step.

---

## Evidence UX Improvements

Learners can submit `answer` + `confidence` + `approach` without typing.

Approach ids remain `{knew, worked, pattern, guessed, unsure}` and are concatenated into `reasoning` for the frozen analyzer.

The 1–5 engine confidence scale is unchanged. The learner UI offers 1 / 3 / 5.

---

## Challenge Diversity

Product rotation policy:

- same exact challenge: avoid within the last 4 shown when alternatives exist
- same concept: allowed
- same type: avoid consecutive repeats when alternatives exist
- different representation: preferred
- repeats allowed for `REMEDIATE` or when the eligible bank is exhausted

The UI does not choose the next challenge.
